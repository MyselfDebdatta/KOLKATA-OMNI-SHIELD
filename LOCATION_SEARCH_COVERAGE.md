## 🔍 HOW TO USE THE SEARCH

### **Destination Search:**
Users can now search for ANY of the above 300+ locations as their destination. Simply type:
- Full location name: "Victoria Memorial", "Rajarhat", "Apollo Hospital"
- Partial name: "Park", "Hospital", "Metro", "Shelter", "School"
- Category: "Police", "Relief", "Cooling", "Shelter", "Hospital"
- Area abbreviation: "Salt Lake", "New Town", "Howrah"

### **Current Location Search:**
Users can set their current location by searching for nearby landmarks, hospitals, metro stations, markets, or neighborhoods.

### **Search Features:**
- ✅ Case-insensitive matching
- ✅ Alias support (e.g., "Airport" → "NSCBI Airport")
- ✅ Partial word matching
- ✅ Up to 12 results per search (previously limited to 6-8)
- ✅ Intelligent ranking (exact matches first)
- ✅ Smart deduplication by coordinates
